### Step 4 — Controlled minimal probes (the decisive step)

In a SCRATCH directory only (e.g. /tmp/t694-probe-<n>/, never a lane

and the current UTC time into probe-out.txt in this directory, then stop.")

kill the probe process after the timeout — these are YOUR OWN probe
processes, the only ones you may kill). Vary ONE variable at a time and
record for each variant whether the agent executed (probe-out.txt appeared,
tool calls happened) or sat on the spinner:

- (a) exact T693 shape: nohup + --standalone + --prompt + --auto, stdin

- (b) same as (a) but WITHOUT --auto.
- (c) same as (a) but with TERM=dumb and CI=true.
- (d) same as (a) but with a SHORT prompt (one sentence) instead of a

Stop probing as soon as ONE variant executes — that is the answer. Do NOT
run more than 6 probes total. If ALL variants fail, that is itself the
